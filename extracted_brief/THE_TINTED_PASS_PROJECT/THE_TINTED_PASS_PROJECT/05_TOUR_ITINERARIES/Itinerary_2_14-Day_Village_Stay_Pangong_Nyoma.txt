Practical notes
\177
Long driving days are 9, 11 and 13 (roughly 5-7 hours each), each with a sketch stop.
\177
The Pangong to Nyoma road is remote and rough. Confirm the route, permits and conditions with a local operator before committing.
\177
Alchi was chosen for the village stay because it combines the mural site with village life. Stok or Sabu are closer to Leh if you want a shorter drive.
\177
Rest days before Nyoma help with acclimatization.
\177
Photography may be restricted inside Alchi's temples.

Options and extras
\177
A 15th day could go to Nubra or an extra rest day at Pangong.
\177
Collect each artist's statement when they finish a piece, so the magazine is half-built by the end.
\177
Offer poems, essays and reflections as optional prompts on the rest days.
Each day pairs a historic traveler with a place and an art exercise (landscape, still life, models or quick sketches). Artists write a short statement for each finished piece, and the best work and writing go into the year-end magazine. Permit rules, road conditions and prices change, so confirm everything with a local operator before booking.
