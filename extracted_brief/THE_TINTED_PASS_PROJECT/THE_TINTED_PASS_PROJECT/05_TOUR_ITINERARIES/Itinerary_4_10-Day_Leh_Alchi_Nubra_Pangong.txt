Monasteries, a village night, the Nubra dunes and the lake, with no Hemis or Stok
Length
10 days, 9 nights
Regions

Focus
Monastery and mural art, village life, Silk Road history, dunes and the lake

About 4,250 m at Pangong; Nubra is about 3,100 m

Practical notes
\177
This is a full plan with little slack: only day 1 is a true rest day and day 5 is light. There is no free day in Leh.
\177
Nubra comes before Pangong because it is much lower, so artists are better acclimatized for the higher lake.
\177
Long driving days are 6, 8 and 10. Khardung La is about 5,360 m, so keep the pass stop short.
\177
The direct Nubra to Pangong road over the Shyok route can be affected by weather and permits. If it is not open, you must return via Leh, which adds a day. In that case drop Thiksey and Shey or the Likir stop.
\177
Nubra and Pangong need permits; rules differ for Indian and foreign visitors.
\177
Photography may be restricted inside Alchi's temples.
\177
The farewell evening on day 10 depends on an on-time return from Pangong. Have a backup plan for road delays.
Options and extras
\177
Extend to 13 days by restoring the free day in Leh, the Alchi village day and the Likir stop.
\177
Add the Changthang extension (Nyoma or Hanle) as a premium add-on for returning clients.
\177
Collect each artist's statement when they finish a piece, so the magazine is half-built by the end.

\177
Offer poems, essays and reflections as optional prompts on the rest days.
Each day pairs a historic traveler with a place and an art exercise (landscape, still life, models or quick sketches). Artists write a short statement for each finished piece, and the best work and writing go into the year-end magazine. Permit rules, road conditions and prices change, so confirm everything with a local operator before booking.
