15-Day Grand Tour: Ladakh and Changthang
Monasteries first, then Pangong and the high-altitude lakes of Changthang
Length
15 days, 14 nights
Regions
Leh, Alchi and Lamayuru, Pangong Tso, Hanle, Tso Moriri, Tso Kar
Focus
Monastery art, Silk Road history, Changpa nomad life, big landscapes
Highest sleeping altitude
About 4,300 m at Hanle; Tso Moriri and Tso Kar are around 4,500 m

Practical notes
\177
Two Leh days at the start, then monastery work before the high lakes, so artists build skills and acclimatize.
\177
Altitude is the main risk. Sleeping at Hanle and Tso Moriri is tough, so consider an extra rest day before Changthang.
\177
Pangong, Hanle and Tso Moriri need permits; rules differ for Indian and foreign visitors.
\177
Remote stays are basic (camps and homestays). Book early for summer.
\177
Carry all art supplies from Leh; shops further out will not have them.
\177
Heavy driving: this is the most travel-intensive of the three plans.

Options and extras
\177
For 12 days: drop the Alchi-Lamayuru trip and the Pangong return day.
\177
Nubra could be added as a 2-day extension for the Haidar Dughlat link.
\177
Collect each artist's statement when they finish a piece, so the magazine is half-built by the end.
\177
Offer poems, essays and reflections as optional prompts on the rest days.
Each day pairs a historic traveler with a place and an art exercise (landscape, still life, models or quick sketches). Artists write a short statement for each finished piece, and the best work and writing go into the year-end magazine. Permit rules, road conditions and prices change, so confirm everything with a local operator before booking.
