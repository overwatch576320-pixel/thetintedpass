13-Day Tour: Leh, Sham, Nubra and Pangong
Focused on the heartland, with no Changthang leg
Length
13 days, 12 nights
Regions
Leh, Sham valley (Alchi, Likir), Nubra, Pangong Tso
Focus
Monasteries, Silk Road history, dunes and the lake
Highest sleeping altitude
About 4,250 m at Pangong; Nubra is about 3,100 m

Practical notes
\177
Nubra comes before Pangong because it is much lower, so people are better acclimatized for the higher lake.
\177
Khardung La is about 5,360 m, so keep the pass stop short.
\177
The direct Nubra to Pangong road over the Shyok route is long and can be affected by weather and permits. If it is not open, return to Leh and go to Pangong from there; this adds a day.
\177
Nubra and Pangong need permits; rules differ for Indian and foreign visitors.
\177
Long driving days are 8, 10 and 12, each with a sketch stop.
Options and extras
\177
For 10 days: drop the free day and the Likir stop, and merge the two Alchi days into one.

Offer poems, essays and reflections as optional prompts on the rest days.
Each day pairs a historic traveler with a place and an art exercise (landscape, still life, models or quick sketches). Artists write a short statement for each finished piece, and the best work and writing go into the year-end magazine. Permit rules, road conditions and prices change, so confirm everything with a local operator before booking.
